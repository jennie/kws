# Meet the Musicians of the Kitchener-Waterloo Symphony

For generations, the Kitchener-Waterloo Symphony has played an important role in the cultural life of our region, bringing extraordinary live music to audiences throughout Waterloo Region and beyond. At the heart of that tradition are the musicians of the orchestra. Our musicians are accomplished artists who bring exceptional talent, dedication, and artistry to every performance, continuing the Symphony's long and rich history as a world-class orchestra. Meet the musicians who make the Kitchener-Waterloo Symphony what it is today.
